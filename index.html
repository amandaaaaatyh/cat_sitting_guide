Getting In & Out
Entering the house
Step by step
  1. Telegram me when you're about to reach 📱
  2. Tap the panel and the number pad will appear
  3. Type in the passcode
  4. The gate will unlock, and the main door will unlock shortly after
  5. ⚠️ Push the main door with your leg right after opening the gate — it locks back quite quickly!
Exiting the house
Step by step
  1. The key is in the nook above the oven — it's the non-labelled one
  2. 🟡 The yellow labelled key next to it is for the letter box, not the door!
  3. Open the door
  4. Insert the key into the gate lock
  5. Turn anticlockwise
  6. Once the gate is unlocked, place the key back in the nook 🔑
